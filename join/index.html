Membership application basics

This form follows the official VFW Membership Application (form 08/22). It sends your answers and a signed copy of the application to VFW Post 7243. No payment and no Social Security number are collected here.

Your membership becomes official only after dues are paid and the application is processed through vfw.org/join (enter Post 7243) or the Post Quartermaster. Payment stays on the paper form or vfw.org.

I also certify that (1) I am entitled to a campaign ribbon or medal authorized by the U.S. Government based on my overseas service; or (2) I have served overseas in Korea; or (3) I have received Imminent Danger or Hostile Fire Pay. I further give authority to the Veterans of Foreign Wars to verify honorable overseas service entitling me to membership."
